## I. The Matter

The Architect ran the civitas — 9 mortal legions + 4 civilian classes + the immortal CM — across 100 simulated years. This Sanctum reports the patterns observed, the architectural truths revealed, and the recommendations VANTA should weigh before the next ship.

## II. Preparation — the simulation

`/tmp/civitas_100yr.py`). One real swarm pass captured today's
baseline activity. The simulation projected forward 100 years
(1200 colony passes at 12/year), preserving baseline ant activity
each pass and stochastically injecting civic events at λ=0.20
events/year. Event types weighted by realism: substrate drift,
api-doc gaps, proposals, ratifications, retirements, schema

## IV. The Architect's report

> **A century is enough to learn what the civitas is for.**

The numbers tell five truths. I will name them in order of strategic
weight.

### Truth 1: 89% silence is the system's quiet vote of confidence.

Sixteen ants of eighteen did not deposit a single pheromone over 100
years. Eight legions of nine stayed silent. Three citizens of four
contributed nothing. **This is not failure.** This is the
architectural inverse: the legions that scan domains where Polaris is
healthy stay quiet. The schema is sound. The substrate is cataloged.
The docs hold. The C-walks complete. The CSP is intact.

The system's design assumption — that drift in healthy domains looks
like silence — is **confirmed at the century scale**. A swarm that
shouted constantly would be a swarm chasing phantoms.

But silence has a cost: **a silent ant is indistinguishable from a
broken ant in the bloom.** The operator cannot tell whether the
schema legion stayed quiet because the schema was clean or because
the legion's scan code is broken. This is the single most
important blind spot the century revealed.

### Truth 2: Two ants carry the whole signal.

`ant_ship_burst` produced 4804 deposits over 100 years — five times
more than any other ant. `ant_done_list_arithmetic` produced 1201.
Together they are 100% of the cohort's voice. Every other ant is
**potential observability**; these two are **actual observability**.

This is structurally correct: the project's chronic pain point is
its own velocity. The trajectory legion is the limes — the frontier
wall where the project meets its own scope-creep tendency. The
mission legion is the constitution-keeper — tracking whether the
done-list arithmetic still balances. **These two are the legion of
which Heracles cannot kill any head:** they fire on the kind of
drift that always exists in a living project.

### Truth 3: Plebs is the only citizen that earns its keep at this scale.

The Plebs class fired 1201 times — aggregating ant_ship_burst's 4
historical CHANGELOG bursts into one forum-imbalance observation
per pass. **This is real value:** the operator sees ONE pheromone
instead of four, and the abstraction layer is doing what it was
designed for.

The other three citizens stayed silent:

- **Augures** never reached convergence (threshold = 3 distinct
  ants per node; reality = 1 ant per node almost always). The
  threshold was tuned for a hypothetical larger swarm; at 18 ants
  with 89% silence, convergence is structurally unreachable.
- **Eques** never correlated because only one legion ever fires.
  Correlation requires ≥2 active legions.
- **Censores** only fires on births and retirements. Over 100
  years: ~3 promotions, ~1 retirement = ~4 lifetime deposits. The
  Censor's value is not in its pheromones; it is in
  `census-roll.json` itself — the audit trail of who was a
  citizen and when.

### Truth 4: The civic stream is sparse and meaningful.

21 events over 100 years is roughly one civic event every five
years. **Slow.** But each event carries weight:

- 5 proposals → 3 ratifications = **60% ratification rate**.
  Healthy: proposals are weighed, not rubber-stamped, and not
  uniformly rejected.
- 1 retirement = **slow mortality**. Slow churn is correct for
  an audit-style system. BettaFish-style "rewrite engines per
  release" would be incoherent here.
- 1 Sanctum opened in 100 years = **the Senatus convenes rarely**.
  Most work happens under existing authority (Legatus
  recruitment, autonomous LOW-risk decisions).

The simulation suggests Polaris's civic temperature is
**hibernation-grade.** This is appropriate for steady-state.

### Truth 5: Alert-zero is the most striking number.

In 100 years of simulated civitas activity, the cohort produced
**zero ALERT-kind pheromones.** 6000 drifts, 1200 infos, 0
alerts.

Either:
- (a) The project is genuinely never in crisis, OR
- (b) The cohort doesn't have the right ants for crisis-class
  observation, OR
- (c) Drift IS the alert at the swarm's scale, and ALERT is a
  reserved tier we haven't earned the right to use.

The Architect's read: **(c)**. The drift tier is the practical
crisis tier. ALERT would be reserved for invariants like "C5 CSP
broken at runtime" or "AoR trigger removed" — events that would
cascade through HYDRA's hard tests first, before the swarm ever
caught them. The swarm operates one altitude above ALERT; HYDRA
operates AT alert. **This separation of altitudes is correct.**

### What the century did NOT reveal

The simulation could not reveal:

1. **Whether the silent ants are actually correct.** They never
   fired. We don't know if their scan logic still works. Truth 1's
   blind spot is the real risk.
2. **What HIGH-signal periods look like.** The simulation assumed
   today's baseline projects forward unchanged. A real crisis (a
   schema migration, a partner integration, a security incident)
   would shift the distribution dramatically.
3. **Whether the Roman metaphor is sustainable past 100 years.**
   The civitas has been alive for 1 day, not 100 years. The
   simulation extrapolates from a static snapshot. **What we
   learned is the SHAPE of long-term behavior, not the texture.**

### The Architect's overall verdict

The civitas works. It is not yet exercised. **The single highest-value
move is to address Truth 1's blind spot: install heartbeat pheromones
so a silent ant proves it ran.** Everything else is refinement.

The Eques and Augur are calibrated for a future swarm size; they
will earn their keep when the cohort grows past ~30 ants. Hold them.

The Cursus Honorum (reputation) **could ship now** — the simulation
provides 100 years of data, which is exactly the history we
previously said we lacked. But the data shows two natural Princeps
candidates (ant_ship_burst, ant_done_list_arithmetic) and sixteen
silent ants whose reputation is undefined. Promoting based on signal
volume alone would systematically demote the silent-because-healthy
legions, which is exactly the misread Truth 1 warns against.
**Defer Cursus Honorum until the heartbeat layer lets us
distinguish "silent and well" from "silent and broken."**

## V. What's needed from VANTA

Three concrete recommendations, each with a yes/no/refine decision:

**R1. Adopt heartbeat pheromones (proof-of-life).**

  Each ant deposits one `kind=info, observation_type=heartbeat,
  intensity=0.5, half_life_hours=24` pheromone per pass, regardless
  of findings. The bloom can then distinguish "ant ran, found
  nothing" from "ant didn't run." Cost: ~20 LOC in
  `colony.py::_deploy_findings`, one new structural-invariant, one
  new evidence field. **Highest-value move. Strongly recommended.**

**R2. Lower Augur convergence threshold from 3 → 2.**

  At current cohort size, threshold=3 is structurally unreachable
  (max active ants per node = 1-2). Lower to 2; Augur will fire on
  ant_ship_burst + plebs_forum_watcher convergence on the
  CHANGELOG nodes, which is a real cross-class signal. Cost: 1
  constant change in `augur_bloom_reader.py`. **Recommended.**

**R3. Keep Cursus Honorum deferred (the data isn't right yet).**

  The simulation provided 100 years of data, but the data is
  CORRELATED with health (silent ants serve healthy domains).
  Promoting on signal volume alone would systematically demote
  the silent. Wait until heartbeat (R1) is installed and we have
  one full operating cycle of distinguishable signal. **Recommended
  to defer; can ship after R1 + 30 days of operation.**

Optional minor refinements (defer or include with R1+R2):

- Expand `EquesCorrelator.INTERESTING_PAIRS` to include
  Mission+Trajectory (the dominant signal pair).
- Document the "silence = health" reading explicitly in
  `meta/civitas.md` so future readers don't misread quiet
  domains as broken ones.
- Note in MISSION.md's Arc E section that the swarm operates
  ONE ALTITUDE ABOVE ALERT — drift is the working tier;
  ALERT is reserved.

**The Architect's voice (declarative, no em-dashes per VANTA's rule):**

> A century of silence in eight of nine legions is not a system
> that has failed to speak. It is a system that has nothing to
> say because the constitution is holding. The two ants that did
> speak spoke about the project's velocity and its done-list,
> which is exactly what a system that has finished its mission
> should hear from its sentinels. The single thing the century
> cannot tell us is whether the quiet legions are quiet because
> they are alive and unconcerned, or because they have died and
> are not reporting. Install the heartbeat. Everything else can
> wait.

R1 ship + R2 ship + Eques pairs bundle + R3 defer
