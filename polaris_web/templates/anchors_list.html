How anchor batches work

  1. Pending pool: BlockchainAnchor rows accumulate with batch_id IS NULL.
  2. Close ceremony: the close_anchor_batch(algorithm_id, root, proofs) procedure takes a pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext('polaris.anchor.close-batch.' || algorithm_id)), computes the Merkle root server-side via polaris_web/anchoring.py, and assigns the pending rows.
  3. Verification: /api/anchor/verify/<token_id> reconstructs the root from leaf + proof. Tampered logs are rejected at the API boundary, not in the client.
  4. Operator discretion: committed_to_chain is operator-set, not auto-derived. The schema implements the primitive; whether and when to publish to Algorand-PQ, Hyperledger Indy, or a custom lattice is the operator's call.

Mechanism design: DEVNOTES/ships/anchoring.md. Concurrency: per-algorithm lock is the 4th entry in DEVNOTES/concurrency.md.
