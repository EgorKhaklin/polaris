Use Case 9 — Phase 1

Initiate Catastrophic-Loss Recovery

Out-of-band recovery ceremony for the case PDF §9.1 names: a holder loses ALL of their tokens and devices simultaneously. This is phase 1 — open a PENDING request. No token is issued here. A different administrator must complete phase 2 after the 48-hour cool-down and after all three out-of-band channels (biometric, sworn statement, witness agency co-sign) are verified. Implements R11-2 / M2-7. The third leg of the "schema doesn't weaponize itself against the holder" triad.

The agency on whose behalf the recovery is being initiated.

What happens after submit:

  1. A RecoveryRequest row is created with status=PENDING.
  2. The 48-hour cool-down clock starts.
  3. Out-of-band verification (biometric, sworn statement, witness agency) happens externally.
  4. A different administrator reviews the queue (/uc9/queue) and decides APPROVED or REJECTED.

On APPROVED, the procedure transitions all of the individual's non-terminal tokens to LOST (publishing each to RevocationList) and issues a new ACTIVE token with predecessor_token_id=NULL. All transitions are audit-tagged [RECOVERY:<id>] in the lifecycle log.
