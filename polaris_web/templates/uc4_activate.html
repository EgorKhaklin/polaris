Use Case 4

Reserve Token Activation After Loss

Atomic operation wrapping uc4_activate_reserve. Transitions a lost token to its terminal status (LOST or REVOKED depending on reason), publishes the revocation, and promotes the holder's RESERVE token to ACTIVE with predecessor_token_id pointing to the lost token. The order (terminal first, then promote) keeps the partial unique index satisfied without deferred-constraint semantics.
