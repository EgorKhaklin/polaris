Use Case 8

Bounded Revocation

Atomic operation wrapping uc8_revoke_token. Transitions the token to REVOKED and publishes a RevocationList row in the same transaction. Enforces a per-agency rolling-window rate bound (system default 5% of an agency's outstanding tokens per 30 days). Above the bound a co-signer is required: the co-signer must hold BOTH on the token's algorithm and must differ from the actor. This is the constitutional limit on issuer discretion: no single agency can revoke faster than its declared bound without a second signature.
