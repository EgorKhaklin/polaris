ZK Epochs

Plonky2 ZK-SNARK epochs. Each closed epoch carries a Merkle root the prover commits to; the SNARK proves Merkle inclusion of a token leaf into that root, bound to (epoch_id, context_id, nonce) as public inputs. Closing is operator-initiated via uc11_close_epoch under a per-procedure advisory lock.

How epochs prove inclusion

  1. Closure: uc11_close_epoch takes a list of (token_id, leaf_hash, proof_path) tuples and a precomputed merkle_root, then writes one TokenStateEpoch + N TokenStateEpochLeaf rows under a per-procedure pg_advisory_xact_lock.
  2. Public inputs: the Plonky2 circuit binds the proof to (epoch_id, context_id, nonce). Replay with a different nonce fails verification. Cross-epoch substitution fails verification.
  3. Substrate: Plonky2 is FRI-based (post-quantum-comfortable). The Rust crate polaris_zk/ is the prover/verifier; polaris_web/zk.py is the Python subprocess wrapper.
